# S5a: Insights

Brief per `docs/ENGINEERING.md` §5.1. S5 in `BUILD_PLAN.md` is split (owner: small, frequent releases): **S5a Insights** (this brief: the month so far, the Insights tab, notes, fairness, the standing-order hint) and **S5b** (the monthly recap, settle day, year in review, on this day, comments). Ask smsm (INS-9) is S6 (it needs the AI). Process: every figure is money, so the full protocol (tests first by a separate agent, fresh review).

## Goal
Without a spreadsheet, the two of you can see how the month is going against your usual, where the money went, how it trends, who has been paying, and whether a standing order would keep you square. Every figure is the core's, exact to the penny, and can be tapped for its one-line definition.

## Criteria in scope
INS-1, INS-3, INS-4, INS-5, INS-6; CHP-5 (usual figures use everyday spending only; totals can include chapters); PLC-7 (Insights by country).

## Definitions (DATA_MODEL §3 and §5; the core implements exactly these)
- **Shared spend** for a period: live `expense` rows with `split_mode` in (ratio, exact, treat), full `amount_pence`, on their `date`, minus live `money_back` rows in the period (any split mode except for_other). Excludes `for_other` and settle-ups. By category: the same per category (money back counts against its own category).
- **Everyday**: `chapter_id` null. "Usual", pace and notes use everyday only. The Insights tab's totals include chapters by default, with a switch "Include chapters" (on) to see everyday only.
- **Usual** (monthly): median of the previous 3–6 full calendar months of everyday shared spend; fewer than 3 full months of history before this month: not shown. "Full month": a month after the first month with any entry. Median of an even count: the mean of the two middle values, rounded down to the penny.
- **Pace** (INS-1): this month's everyday spend to date ÷ (usual × days elapsed ÷ days in month) − 1. Home line: "£1,240.00 so far" plus, when usual exists, "· about £80 under your usual pace" / "over" / "· on your usual pace" (within £10). The amount is |this month to date − usual × days elapsed ÷ days in month|, rounded to the nearest £10 (halves up), shown as "£80".
- **Notes** (INS-3), each stating its rule, at most one on Home (the largest by money), all on Insights:
  - unusual category: this month ≥ 1.3 × its usual and ≥ £20 above → "Eating out is £85 over its usual (£210 so far, usually £125)";
  - unusual entry: ≥ 3 × its category's median over the previous 6 months, with ≥ 5 earlier entries in that category → "Big shop £180 is about 3× a usual Groceries entry";
  - a subscription whose price changed this month (from the template's history) → "Netflix went from £10.99 to £12.99";
  - a new regular payment started this month → "New regular payment: Gym £30.00 a month".
- **Who paid**: Σ `paid[person]` over shared spending in the period (money back: minus what each received).
- **Fairness** (INS-5), over the last 3 and 12 months (to today): each person's paid ÷ total paid, as whole percentages (largest remainder, ties to the first person), beside the household's agreed split ("Jae paid 58%, Sam 42% · your split is 50/50"). No judgement words.
- **Standing-order hint** (INS-6): the month-end net balance for each of the last 3 full months; if the same person owed at all three ends and the mean of those amounts is ≥ £50, a note: "Sam has owed Jae at the end of each of the last 3 months, about £120 on average. A standing order of £120 a month would keep you about square." Amount: the mean rounded down to £10.
- **Trend**: 12 calendar months ending with this one, shared spend per month (with the switch).
- **By country** (PLC-7): shared spend per country in the period, shown only when more than one country.
- **Periods**: This month, Last 3 months, Last 12 months, This year, and each chapter. "Comparison": the same-length period just before ("£2,340.00, £120.00 less than the 3 months before").

## Screens
- **Insights tab** (the fourth tab appears): period selector; one sentence and one chart at a time, scrolled: total and comparison; where it went (horizontal bars labelled on the bars); trend (12 slim columns, the current month marked); who paid; fairness; by country (only if more than one); notes. Charts are hand-drawn SVG with direct labels, no legends, a baseline only; each mark can be tapped for its figure. Every figure has a "What's this?" definition.
- **Home**: the month-so-far line under the balance (INS-1) and at most one note.

## Data / core
- Core `src/core/insights.ts` (pure, property-tested): `sharedSpend(entries, period, { everydayOnly })`, `byCategory`, `byCountry`, `whoPaid`, `monthlyTotals(entries, months)`, `usual(entries, month)`, `pace(entries, today)`, `notes(input, today)`, `fairness(entries, people, period, split)`, `standingOrder(entries, settlements, people, today)`, `periodOf(choice, today, chapter?)`, `previousPeriod(period)`.
- No new tables; no new packages (charts are SVG by hand).

## Tests
Core: vectors from DATA_MODEL (shared spend excludes for_other and settle-ups; money back subtracts; everyday only for usual/notes), usual (3–6 months, median, fewer than 3 → none), pace sign and rounding, each note rule and its edge (exactly 1.3× and £20; exactly 3× with 5 earlier), fairness percentages sum to 100, standing order (three same-direction months, mean ≥ £50, rounded down to £10), properties (by-category and who-paid totals equal the total). Screens: Insights tab and periods; Home line. E2E: Insights at 320/390, both themes, axe.

## Interfaces (what the tests are written against)
- Tabs: "Home", "Activity", "Bills", "Insights" (the fourth); route `/insights`; h1 "Insights".
- Period selector: radiogroup "Period" with radios "This month", "Last 3 months", "Last 12 months", "This year", plus a combobox or button "Chapter" listing chapters (when any).
- Switch or checkbox "Include chapters" (on by default).
- Regions: "Total" (the sentence, e.g. "£2,340.00 this month" / "£2,340.00, £120.00 less than the 3 months before"), "Where it went" (a list of categories "Eating out £420.00"), "Trend" (12 columns, each a button or listitem named "Nov 2026: £2,340.00"), "Who paid" ("Jae £1,200.00"), "Fairness" (the sentence), "By country" (only with more than one), "Notes" (list).
- "What's this?" buttons open a small sheet (dialog) named after the figure with its definition.
- Home: region "This month" with "£1,240.00 so far" and, when usual exists, "· about £80 under your usual pace"; at most one note.
- Core exports as listed above (shapes are the test writer's to pin, documented in the test file header).
