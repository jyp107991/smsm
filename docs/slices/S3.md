# S3: recurring, bills, inbox, categories

Brief per `docs/ENGINEERING.md` §5.1. Status: started 17 Nov, after the S2 polish. Process: money, saving and syncing parts take the full protocol (tests first by a separate agent, fresh review); screens and wording take the owner's lighter process (DECISIONS, 17 Nov).

## Goal
Regular shared costs look after themselves: rent and subscriptions post on their day, once, from whichever phone opens first or the nightly job if neither does; bills whose amount changes ask for the amount in an Inbox instead of guessing; a Bills tab shows the month, what's coming, subscriptions and what home costs; categories can be edited.

## Criteria in scope
REC-1, REC-2 (posting by the database and the nightly job; the dates already agree with SQL, S1), REC-3, REC-4, REC-5, REC-6, REC-7, REC-8, INB-1 (variable bills only; the other sources arrive with S6/S7/later, but the Inbox is built for them), INB-2 (Home count; the icon badge waits for S8), INB-3, CAT-1. PPL-5's default-split approval was built in S2; S3 only checks that the Bills "usual split" for a new template follows SPL-2.

## Out of scope
Notifications for a posted bill (S8, NOT-1 as the owner changed it), chapters and countries beyond "always home" (S4), Insights (S5), receipts and AI (S6), import (S7).

## Screens
- **Bills tab** (the third tab appears): this month as a calendar (posted and upcoming days marked, the month's total), "Next 30 days" as a list, **Subscriptions** (each active one with its monthly and yearly cost, and a yearly total), and **What home costs** ("£2,140 a month to run"). Button "Add a regular payment".
- **Regular payment sheet** (also reached from the Add sheet's "More details" → "Repeat"): the usual entry fields plus How often (weekly, monthly, yearly, every N), Starts, Ends (optional), "Amount varies", "Subscription". Editing changes future occurrences only (REC-6); the history line records price changes ("Rent £1,450 → £1,500 from March"). Pause (inactive) and end; nothing is hard-deleted.
- **Inbox** (a row on Home with its count when not empty; a page listing items): "Energy is due: how much was it?" with the usual amount offered → **Keep** opens the Add sheet prefilled (saving makes it an entry and marks the item kept) or **Not ours** (dismissed). Never counted in any figure (INB-3).
- **Suggest recurring** (REC-5): when a description has appeared three times at a steady interval with similar amounts, offer once: "Netflix looks monthly. Make it a regular payment?" Yes opens the sheet prefilled; No never asks again for that description.
- **Categories** (Settings): rename, emoji, colour from the palette, reorder, archive (history kept), add.

## Data
- `recurring`, `inbox_items`, `post_due_recurring(household)` and the posting trigger/logging already exist in the baseline (tested in `tests/sql/`). Check them against REC-1…REC-6 and extend the SQL tests rather than rewriting.
- **Nightly job:** `pg_cron` in the database (`select post_due_recurring(null)` for every household, daily early morning UK time) — no secret leaves the database. Migration enables the extension and schedules it; a SQL test proves the job exists and that posting is idempotent when a phone and the job run together.
- The app calls `post_due_recurring()` after sign-in and on the first open of each day (already wired in `src/data/queryClient.ts`; verify).
- New data-layer hooks and mutations: `useRecurring`, `useInbox`, `saveRecurring` (upsert by phone-made id), `pauseRecurring`/`endRecurring`, `keepInboxItem`, `dismissInboxItem`, `saveCategory`, `archiveCategory`, `reorderCategories` — all through the persisted queue, offline-safe, realtime-synced.
- Core: `monthlyCost(schedule, amount)` for REC-8 (weekly × 52 ÷ 12 ÷ every; yearly ÷ 12 ÷ every; integer pence, rounding stated in DECISIONS), `suggestRecurring(history)` for REC-5 (pure, property-tested), upcoming occurrences for the calendar (reuse `core/recurring`).

## Tests
- SQL: posting once under concurrency; amount-varies goes to the inbox, never to expenses; always home country and no chapter; editing a template doesn't touch posted occurrences; the cron job exists.
- Core: monthly cost, suggest-recurring (vectors + properties), upcoming list.
- Data layer: offline template save then sync; inbox keep/dismiss once (double tap safe, Q-F1 style ids).
- Two-user e2e: a template due today posts once and both phones see it; an amount-varies bill appears in both Inboxes, one person keeps it, the other's Inbox clears; balance on screen = core = `member_net` after each.
- Demo e2e: Bills tab at 320/390, both themes, axe clean; Inbox; categories editor.

## Risks
- Double posting when a phone and the nightly job run at once: the function locks the template row (`for update`) — prove it in SQL with two sessions.
- Time zones: occurrences are UK dates (DAT-2); a phone abroad still posts on the UK date.
- Owner's real data is live: migrations must be additive; no rewrite of existing rows.

## Interfaces (what the tests are written against)
Written with the tests first (17 Nov). Copy may be refined by the voice work (DESIGN §5), but these accessible names, functions and values are the contract; change them only together with the tests. Tests: `tests/sql/recurring_test.sql`, `tests/sql/categories_test.sql`, `src/core/bills.test.ts` (+ `tests/vectors/bills.json`), `src/data/bills.test.ts`, `src/screens/bills.test.tsx`, `tests/e2e/bills.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/two-users/recurring.spec.ts`.

**Routes**: `/bills` (the Bills tab), `/inbox`, `/categories` (reached from Settings). The Bills tab is the third tab ("Home", "Activity", "Bills"); `FEATURES.bills` turns on.

**Database** (a new additive migration; existing rows untouched)
- `post_due_recurring` posts by the **UK date**, `(now() at time zone 'Europe/London')::date`, whatever the session's time zone (DAT-2); each occurrence once when two phones, or a phone and the nightly job, call at once (`posted_count` counts each once); fixed amounts → expenses (`source 'recurring'`, `recurring_id`, `occurrence_date`, the household's home country, no chapter, REC-4); amount varies → one open `inbox_items` row per occurrence with payload `{ description, estimate_pence, date, category_id }` and never an expense.
- Nightly job: pg_cron job named **`smsm-recurring`**, scheduled daily between 00:00 and 05:59 UTC (`m h * * *` with h 0–5: after midnight in London in winter and summer), its command calling `post_due_recurring(id)` for every household (e.g. `select post_due_recurring(id) from households`). The migration enables `pg_cron` only where it is available and schedules through `cron.schedule('smsm-recurring', …)` whenever a `cron` schema exists. Plain PostgreSQL 16 (`npm run test:sql`) has no pg_cron, so `tests/sql/auth_stub.sql` creates a stand-in `cron` schema there (`cron.job`, `cron.schedule`, `cron.unschedule`, upserting by job name like the real one); the real extension is checked on CI's Supabase stack by `tests/e2e/two-users/recurring.spec.ts`.
- `recurring` and `inbox_items` join the `supabase_realtime` publication.
- Templates are never hard-deleted: `delete` on `recurring` is not granted (pause = `active false`, end = `end_date`).
- Categories: `colour` must be one of the palette names `moss, amber, walnut, honey, slate, plum, oak, berry, lake, clay, fern, stone`; `delete` on `categories` is not granted (archive instead); a category change is logged in `activity` with `entity 'category'` (an edit's summary is `{ field: { before, after } }`, as for expenses).
- A kept Inbox bill is an expense carrying the item's `recurring_id` and `occurrence_date`, so the existing unique index refuses a second keep of the same bill from the other phone.

**Core** (`src/core/recurring.ts`, pure)
- `monthlyCost({ frequency, every }, amount)`: weekly amount×52÷12÷every; monthly amount÷every; yearly amount÷12÷every. `yearlyCost`: weekly ×52÷every; monthly ×12÷every; yearly ÷every. **Rounding: worked out exactly, rounded once to the nearest penny, halves up** (Prime £95.00 a year → £7.92 a month; fortnightly 3p → 7p a month). `every` < 1 or not whole throws RangeError. "What home costs" = the sum of the rounded monthly costs of the templates that count (below), so the figures shown add up.
- `occurrencesBetween(schedule, from, to, fromIndex = 0)`; `upcoming(templates, { from?, to })`: the occurrences not yet posted (k ≥ posted_count) of active templates, not after their end date, by date then description; without `from` an overdue one that couldn't post yet (offline) is included.
- `suggestRecurring(history, today, exclude?)` (REC-5): grouped by `normaliseKey(description)`; the **last three** entries must fall on three different days with both gaps in one window — **6–8 days → weekly; 13–15 → every 2 weeks; 26–35 → monthly; 86–96 → every 3 months; 358–372 → yearly** — with amounts **within 10 %** (10·max ≤ 11·min), and the last one **recent** (today − last ≤ the window's upper bound). Offers the latest description as typed and its amount, `lastDate`, `nextDate` (one step after the last); most recent first. The caller passes live expenses that are not money back and not posted by a template, and excludes descriptions that already have a template or were answered "No".

**A template "counts"** (in What home costs, Subscriptions, the calendar's upcoming days and Next 30 days) when it is active (not paused) and still has an occurrence to come on or before its end date. Ending a template (`end_date` = today, its next occurrence after that) takes it out at once.

**Data layer** (`src/data/`; full signatures in the header of `src/data/bills.test.ts`)
- Hooks: `useRecurring()` (every template, waiting writes applied), `useInbox()` (open items only, oldest first; a decision on this phone removes the item at once).
- Writes, all through the persisted queue in order with the money writes, offline-safe, counted in "changes waiting to sync": `saveRecurring(draft & { id? })` (upsert by phone-made id; paid and shares built by the core; never sends `posted_count`; once stored the phone calls `post_due_recurring()` so a template due today posts at once), `pauseRecurring`, `resumeRecurring`, `endRecurring(id, endDate = today)`, `keepInboxItem(itemId, expenseDraft & { id? })` (the entry, then the item marked kept with its id; double tap safe), `dismissInboxItem`, `saveCategory({ name, emoji, colour } & { id? })`, `archiveCategory(id, archived = true)`, `reorderCategories(ids)`. Pause, resume, end, dismiss and archive return whether anything changed.
- Api: `recurring(hid)`, `inboxItems(hid)`, `upsertRecurring(row)`, `patchInboxItem(id, patch)`, `upsertCategory(row)`; watched tables gain `recurring` and `inbox_items`. The in-memory Api gains tables `recurring` and `inboxItems`, option `today`, and a `postDueRecurring` that does what the SQL function does. The demo seed passes its templates to it and leaves the latest energy bill open in the Inbox.
