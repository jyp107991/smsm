## Checked facts the design relies on (November 2026)
- Monzo developer API: read accounts (including joint), balances, transactions; webhooks on new transactions; receipts; pots. No payments to other people. Personal use, few users. Full history only in the first 5 minutes after approval, then 90 days. Pot-linked virtual-card spending not exposed.
- monzo.me links accept an amount and a description (`monzo.me/<name>/<amount>?d=<text>`) and open the Monzo app for Monzo users.
- Gemini API: Flash and Flash-Lite models have a free tier with daily limits that vary by project; UK-facing apps are required to use paid services; UK users get paid-service data terms either way.
- Android Chrome installed apps support manifest shortcuts, share target, push and icon badges.
- BigDataCloud offers a free, keyless reverse-geocoding endpoint for calls made from the user's device.
- Supabase free plan: 500 MB database, 1 GB files, 5 GB transfer, pauses after 7 days idle, no backups; hence the keep-alive and off-site backups.
- Supabase: the `anon` and `service_role` keys are being retired at the end of 2026; use the publishable key in the app and secret keys on the server.
Re-check each at the slice that uses it.

| 2026-11-17 | **The write queue's durability:** money writes are TanStack mutations keyed `['money']`, one shared scope (sent one at a time, in order), registered with `setMutationDefaults` before the restore. *Every* waiting write is persisted (not only paused ones: one mid-request when the app closed is replayed, safely, as an upsert by its phone-made id), marked paused so it is resumed after the restore, and written to storage the moment it is queued (the rest of the cache on a 250 ms throttle, and on hide). Restore `maxAge` is unlimited and the cache buster fixed (`smsm-1`): a new app version must never discard the queue; changing the buster needs a migration of the stored queue. Queries never garbage-collect (`gcTime: Infinity`): the household is small, and browser timers longer than about 24.8 days fire at once. Each store has its own `network` (the browser's in the app), not TanStack's browser-wide online flag, so two phones can be simulated side by side; retries of a write happen inside its mutation function (wait, then try again; sooner when the connection returns or the partner joins), so a lost answer is resent with the same id and never taken for a refusal. | OFF-5 "however long they wait"; read from the installed TanStack 5.107 source (`hydration.js`, `mutation.js`, `mutationCache.js`, `retryer.js`). |
| 2026-11-17 | Write failures: no connection, a lost answer, an expired token (401), 408/429/5xx, "this app is too old" and "the version can't be checked" keep the write queued and retry with backoff (1 s doubling to 30 s), for as long as needed; any other refusal is final: the entry leaves the phone's copy and the balance (the rollback), is kept on the phone (`smsm-problems`) with a plain message and what the person entered, for them to fix (edit it by its id) or dismiss, survives restarts, and is reported to `client_errors`; the rest of the queue goes on. | OFF-5: "the person is told and the entry is kept" (resolved with the independent S2 tests). |
